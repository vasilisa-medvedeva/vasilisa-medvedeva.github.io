Spacing

Components sit on a 4px grid. Each step has a job — spacing carries hierarchy the same way the color alpha scale does.

2Micro — inside compact badges (ChipLive, Chip label-to-cancel)
4Icon-to-text gap (VideoChip), tight vertical padding in badges
8Default gap between siblings; small inset (gallery gaps, corner offsets)
12Medium inset — chips, video chip, buttons xs/sm horizontal padding
16Large inset — screen edges, bars, buttons md/lg horizontal padding
24Separation between content blocks inside a screen
32Section breaks — the largest step inside a mobile screen

Vertical paddings inside controls can be odd on purpose — 5, 9 and 18 in Button sizes, 6 in SegmentedControl. They come straight from Figma, where the control's total height has to land on the type grid; the 4px scale governs everything between components, not inside them.
