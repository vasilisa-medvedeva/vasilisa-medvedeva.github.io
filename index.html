Hi! I’ve been working as a product designer for four years.

I create scalable, flexible interfaces that simplify user experiences and help businesses grow — balancing UX, business goals, and technical constraints.

I rapidly test hypotheses and run user interviews to uncover the root causes of behavior, turning insights into clear, valuable solutions.

Work Experience

Pyroblast Product Designer 2023 – 2024

Developed a messenger for gamers (desktop, iOS, Android) from scratch. Conducted user research, built prototypes, tested hypotheses, and was responsible for UX/UI and the design system. Set up Scrum processes within the team, including planning, stand-ups, and task estimation. Coordinated team work and helped prioritize features to ensure consistent product delivery.

Drom Product Designer (iOS and Android) 2019 – 2022

Worked on an application for driving theory learning and exam preparation. Redesigned the main screen from a static layout into a scrollable, modular one, opening the app up to new sections and cross-promo. The PDD app consistently ranked among the top positions on Google Play and reached over 10 million downloads.

Also worked on a traffic fines payment app. After the payment and registration redesign, payment conversion doubled and fines revenue grew 5×; an A/B-tested onboarding brought 16% more registrations.

At Nomerogram, an app for checking a car’s history by its license plate, I owned the search, history and photo contribution flows. A redesigned photo upload onboarding nearly doubled the number of uploaded photos.

DankoLab Web Designer 2019
