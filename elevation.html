Elevation

Shadows live in tokens, not in hand-written box-shadows. Each level maps to a layer of the interface — the higher the surface floats, the higher its level.

--elevation-0
Flat — surfaces resting on the background, separated by borders only
--elevation-1
Subtle lift — cards resting on the background
--elevation-2
Raised — dropdowns and tooltips
--elevation-3
Floating — FAB, snackbar
--elevation-3-up
Same level, shadow cast upward — bottom sheets rising from the bottom edge
--elevation-4
Overlay — dropdown menus, bottom sheets
--elevation-5
Top layer — dialogs and full-screen overlays
